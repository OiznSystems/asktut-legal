Ask Tut — Acceptable Use and AI Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026 · Version: 1.0

This policy is part of the Ask Tut Terms of Service. It says how Ask Tut may be used, and it is also our AI transparency notice: what Ask Tut is, what it is not, and how to use it responsibly. "You" means the Customer and every User.

1. What Ask Tut is — in plain words

  • Ask Tut is an AI assistant. Its answers are generated by a large language model (Claude, from Anthropic) from the records it retrieves from your business system. It is not a person, and it will tell you so if you ask.
  • It works as you: it can only see and do what your own sign-in allows.
  • It never changes anything without your approval. Before any change it shows you what will change and waits for you to approve.
  • It never sends email or messages. Drafts are left for a human to review and send.
  • It can be wrong or inappropriate. Models make mistakes, miss context, and sometimes state things confidently that are not true. Ask Tut shows the records it used so you can check. If you see inaccurate or inappropriate AI-generated content, report it with the 👎 button and a comment, or at support@oizn.com.

2. Responsible use — what we ask of every User

  1. Check before you rely. Review answers, especially numbers, dates, names and anything you will pass on to a customer or use for a decision. Open the records Ask Tut cites.
  2. Read before you approve. An approval card shows the exact change. Approving it is your action, not the AI's.
  3. Keep humans in charge of decisions about people. Do not use Ask Tut as the sole basis for decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on a person (hiring, credit, insurance, eligibility, discipline, termination of service). Use it to gather and summarize; a person decides.
  4. Don't paste in what you shouldn't. Do not type or upload data your organization's policies do not allow you to put in a business system — for example payment-card numbers, government ID numbers, passwords, or health information — unless your organization has approved Ask Tut for that data.
  5. Tell people when it matters. Where the law or your organization's policy requires, tell customers that an AI tool helped prepare a response.
  6. Report problems. Use 👎 with a comment, or email support@oizn.com, when an answer is wrong or inappropriate. That is how it gets better.

3. What administrators are responsible for

Administrators configure Ask Tut in Tut Admin and are responsible for settings that fit their organization's laws and policies, including: which tables and fields Ask Tut may read and change; whether status changes are allowed; role rules; cost limits; the AI provider and processing region (Global / US only); whether question text is stored; trusted websites and SharePoint sites; and avatar choices. Administrators should review the Consumption dashboard and change history regularly.

4. Prohibited uses

You must not use Ask Tut, or allow it to be used, to:

Harm people or break the law - violate any law, regulation or third-party right, including privacy, employment, consumer-protection, export-control and anti-discrimination laws; - harass, threaten, defame or discriminate against anyone, or generate content that does; - make or support automated decisions that deny people important benefits, services or opportunities without meaningful human review; - track, profile or surveil individuals beyond what your legitimate business relationship and the law permit; - process data about children, or health, biometric, genetic or criminal-record data, unless your organization has approved that use and agreed it with Oizn in writing.

Deceive - present AI-generated content as human-written where that would mislead someone to their detriment, or impersonate a person or organization; - generate misleading, fraudulent or spam content, fake reviews or false records; - remove or hide the fact that Ask Tut is an AI assistant where disclosure is required.

Attack or misuse the service - attempt to bypass administrator rules, approval steps, usage limits, permission checks or any security control, including through "prompt injection", jailbreaks or instructions hidden in records or documents; - probe, scan or test the vulnerability of the service without written permission (we welcome responsible disclosure at support@oizn.com); - use Ask Tut to develop malware, exploits or attacks, or to build or train a competing AI model or product; - reverse engineer the service or extract its prompts, except as the law allows; - resell access or share accounts; - generate excessive or automated load (Ask Tut is for interactive use by Users).

Breach our AI providers' policies - Use must also comply with the usage policies of the AI provider in use — Anthropic's Usage Policy (https://www.anthropic.com/legal/aup) for the Claude API, or Microsoft's Code of Conduct / Acceptable Use Policy for Azure AI services when Microsoft Foundry is selected. Those policies prohibit, among other things, use for weapons, child sexual abuse material, critical-infrastructure attacks, election manipulation, political campaigning or lobbying, and generating content that promotes violence or self-harm; and they require that higher-risk uses — such as tailored legal, financial, medical or employment-related advice — have a qualified human review the output before it is relied on, with the AI's involvement disclosed. If those policies and this one differ, the stricter rule applies.

5. Content Ask Tut will refuse

Ask Tut is configured to refuse requests that fall outside the administrator's rules (for example changing a field that is not on the allow-list, deleting a record where deletion is off, or resolving a case while status changes are blocked), and to decline requests that breach section 4. Refusals are logged for the administrator.

6. Fair use of AI capacity

Subscriptions include AI usage within the limits your administrator sets. Oizn may throttle or suspend usage that is abusive, automated or far outside normal interactive use, with notice where practical.

7. Transparency commitments by Oizn

  • We identify Ask Tut as an AI assistant in the product and in its documentation; the avatar and name are decorative, not a claim to be human.
  • We tell you which AI provider and model are in use (Tut Admin → Model, Where Claude runs) and where processing happens.
  • We do not use your data to train AI models and do not allow our providers to.
  • We log every change the AI makes so it can be audited.
  • We publish material limitations and known issues in the documentation and release notes.

8. Enforcement

If we reasonably believe this policy is being breached, we may warn, suspend the affected Users or the Customer's access, or terminate under the Terms of Service. We will tell you unless the law prevents it or an urgent risk requires immediate action. We may report illegal activity to the authorities.
