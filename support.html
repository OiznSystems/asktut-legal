Ask Tut — Support Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026 · Version: 1.0

This is the support page for Ask Tut, published by Oizn Systems Inc. It applies to every Ask Tut edition (Dynamics 365 Customer Service, Finance & Operations, Business Central, SharePoint, HubSpot, Salesforce, and the Ask Tut app for Microsoft Teams), however you obtained it.

How to reach us

Channel Details
Email support@oizn.com — the primary channel; you receive a ticket number by return
Web https://asktut.com/support (this page) — FAQs, status and the contact form
Phone +1 647 388 9889 (Monday–Friday, 9:00–17:00 Eastern Time, excluding Ontario public holidays)
Security issues security@oizn.com — responsible disclosure; please don't post vulnerabilities publicly before we've had a chance to fix them
Privacy requests privacy@oizn.com

Support is provided in English and French.

What's included

Standard support is included in every paid subscription and during trials:

  • help installing, configuring and updating Ask Tut (solutions, apps, Teams package, Tut Admin settings);
  • investigation of errors, wrong behaviour or unexpected answers (please include the request time, the user, and the text from the Consumption dashboard's "latest requests" row if you can);
  • guidance on administrator rules, cost limits, AI provider and processing-region settings;
  • security and privacy questions, DPA and questionnaire support for the Customer's compliance team;
  • product updates and release notes.

Not included: custom development, configuration of your platform beyond Ask Tut, training of your staff beyond the documentation, support for third-party products (Microsoft, HubSpot, Salesforce, Anthropic) — though we'll point you to the right place — and support for modified versions of the Software.

Marketplace note: Ask Tut is an independent application published by Oizn Systems Inc. Microsoft, HubSpot, Salesforce and Anthropic do not provide support for Ask Tut and are not responsible for support requests about it — contact us, not them.

Response targets

Severity Definition First response Workaround or fix target Updates
1 — Critical Ask Tut is down for all users, or a security incident 4 business hours Workaround within 1 business day; fix as soon as possible Every business day until resolved
2 — High A major feature doesn't work for many users, no workaround 1 business day Workaround within 3 business days; fix in the next release Every 2 business days
3 — Normal A feature doesn't work as documented; a workaround exists 2 business days Fix in a scheduled release As progress is made
4 — Low Questions, how-to, feature requests 3 business days Considered for the roadmap —

Business hours: Monday–Friday 9:00–17:00 Eastern Time (Canada). Targets are goals, not guarantees; resolution times depend on the cause (including third-party platforms and AI providers, which we don't control).

Service status and maintenance (hosted edition)

  • Target availability: 99.5% per month, excluding scheduled maintenance.
  • Scheduled maintenance is announced at least 48 hours ahead in Tut Admin and by email to administrators, and is normally done outside North American business hours.
  • Incidents are posted on https://asktut.com/status and, for the affected customers, by email.

Versions and updates

  • Ask Tut is updated continuously (hosted edition) and through solution/app updates (installed components). We support the current version and the previous major version of the installed components for 12 months after a new major version is released.
  • Updates to the installed components are published through the same marketplace or download you used; release notes list changes and any action administrators must take.
  • We follow the platform vendors' supported API versions; when a vendor retires an API, we release an update before the retirement date where the vendor gives us reasonable notice.

What we need from you

To resolve issues quickly, please give us: your organization name and the edition; the environment / portal / org URL; the approximate time of the problem (with time zone); the affected user(s); a screenshot or the text of any error; and, if you can, the request row from the Consumption dashboard. Never send passwords, API keys or payment details.

Data in support tickets

Support correspondence is Account Data under our Privacy Policy. Please avoid including personal information about your customers in tickets; if a problem needs us to see records, we'll agree a secure way to do that with you.

Escalation

If you're not satisfied with how a ticket is handled, reply to the ticket with "escalate" or email mo@oizn.com. Oizn's founder reviews every escalation.
