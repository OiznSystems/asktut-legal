Ask Tut — Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

Effective date: 30 October 2026 · Version: 1.0

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the Ask Tut Terms of Service (the "Agreement") between Oizn Systems Inc. ("Oizn", the "Processor") and the Customer (the "Controller"). It applies whenever Oizn processes Personal Data on the Customer's behalf in providing Ask Tut. Where the Customer runs the customer-hosted edition in its own cloud subscription, Oizn does not process Customer Data and this DPA applies only to any support data the Customer sends Oizn.

1. Definitions

"Personal Data", "processing", "controller", "processor", "data subject", "supervisory authority" and "personal data breach" have the meanings given in the Data Protection Laws. "Data Protection Laws" means all laws that apply to the processing of Personal Data under this DPA, including Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and substantially similar provincial laws (including Quebec's Law 25), the EU General Data Protection Regulation 2016/679 ("GDPR"), the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, the Swiss FADP, and US state privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended ("CCPA"). "Customer Personal Data" means Personal Data in Customer Data that Oizn processes on the Customer's behalf. "Subprocessor" means a third party engaged by Oizn to process Customer Personal Data.

2. Roles and scope

2.1 The Customer is the controller (or, where the Customer is itself a processor, Oizn is a sub-processor) and Oizn is the processor of Customer Personal Data. Oizn is the controller of Account Data as described in the Privacy Policy; that processing is outside this DPA.

2.2 Subject matter, nature and purpose: providing the Ask Tut AI assistant — reading the Customer's platform data as the signed-in user to answer questions, producing documents, applying User-approved changes, logging usage and changes, and support. Duration: the Agreement's term plus the deletion period in section 9. Categories of data subjects and types of Personal Data: Annex 1.

2.3 CCPA. Where the CCPA applies, Oizn is a "service provider". Oizn will not sell or share Customer Personal Data, retain, use or disclose it outside the direct business relationship with the Customer or for any purpose other than the business purposes in this DPA, or combine it with Personal Data from other sources except as the CCPA permits for service providers. Oizn certifies that it understands these restrictions and will comply with them. The Customer may take reasonable and appropriate steps to ensure Oizn uses Customer Personal Data consistently with the Customer's CCPA obligations and, on notice, to stop and remediate any unauthorized use. Oizn will notify the Customer if it determines it can no longer meet its CCPA obligations.

3. Oizn's obligations

Oizn will:

(a) process Customer Personal Data only on the Customer's documented instructions — which are the Agreement, this DPA, the administrator settings in Tut Admin, and Users' questions and approvals — unless required by law, in which case Oizn will tell the Customer before processing (unless the law prohibits that); (b) inform the Customer promptly if it believes an instruction infringes Data Protection Laws; (c) ensure persons authorized to process Customer Personal Data are bound by confidentiality; (d) implement the technical and organizational measures in Annex 3 and keep them under review; (e) assist the Customer, taking into account the nature of the processing, in responding to data-subject requests (section 6), and in meeting its obligations on security, breach notification, data protection impact assessments and prior consultation (sections 7–8); (f) delete or return Customer Personal Data at the end of the services (section 9); (g) make available the information needed to demonstrate compliance and allow audits (section 10); (h) not use Customer Personal Data to train or improve any AI model, and contractually require its AI Subprocessors not to do so.

4. The Customer's obligations

The Customer warrants that it has the legal basis, notices and consents required to let Oizn process Customer Personal Data as described; that its instructions comply with Data Protection Laws; that it will configure Ask Tut (tables, fields, AI provider, processing region, question-text retention, user limits) consistently with its own obligations; and that it will tell Oizn of any special categories of data it intends to process with Ask Tut (Oizn does not design Ask Tut for health, biometric or similar sensitive data and does not sign HIPAA business associate agreements unless agreed separately in writing).

5. Subprocessors

5.1 The Customer gives general authorization to the Subprocessors in Annex 2. Oizn remains responsible for their performance.

5.2 Oizn will give the Customer at least 30 days' notice (by email to the administrator contact and by updating asktut.com/dpa) before adding or replacing a Subprocessor that will process Customer Personal Data. The Customer may object on reasonable data-protection grounds within that period; the parties will discuss in good faith and, if no resolution is found, the Customer may terminate the affected subscription with a pro-rata refund of prepaid fees.

5.3 Oizn will impose data-protection obligations on each Subprocessor that are at least as protective as this DPA.

5.4 AI provider chosen by the Customer. The AI provider (Anthropic via the Claude API) is a Subprocessor only when the Customer's administrator selects it. When the administrator selects Microsoft Foundry in the Customer's own Azure subscription, or the Customer connects its own AI-provider account, that provider processes data under the Customer's own agreement with it and is not Oizn's Subprocessor.

6. Data-subject requests

If Oizn receives a request from a data subject concerning Customer Personal Data, it will not respond except to direct the person to the Customer, and will notify the Customer within 5 business days. Oizn will assist the Customer with access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction and objection requests to the extent the Customer cannot fulfil them itself through Tut Admin or its own platform. Reasonable assistance costs may be charged where requests are excessive.

7. Personal data breach

Oizn will notify the Customer — and specifically the Customer's designated privacy officer or person in charge of the protection of personal information, where one has been named to Oizn — without undue delay and no later than 48 hours after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer Personal Data, with the information reasonably available at the time (nature of the breach, categories and approximate numbers of data subjects and records, likely consequences, measures taken or proposed), and will supplement it as information becomes available. Oizn will cooperate with the Customer's notifications to supervisory authorities and data subjects. Notification is not an admission of fault.

8. Impact assessments

Oizn will provide reasonable information about Ask Tut's processing (including the Security Overview and this DPA) to assist the Customer with data protection impact assessments and prior consultations, and with the Customer's assessment of AI-specific risks.

9. Return and deletion

At the end of the Agreement (or earlier on the Customer's written request), Oizn will make Customer Personal Data it holds available for export in a common format (CSV/Excel/JSON) for 30 days, then delete it from live systems and, within a further 90 days, from backups, unless the law requires retention. During that backup window the data is isolated, put beyond further processing and protected by the measures in Annex 3. Customer Personal Data stored in the Customer's own environment (Dataverse, SharePoint, the Customer's Azure subscription) is under the Customer's control and is not affected. On request, Oizn will confirm deletion in writing.

10. Audits

Oizn will make available, on request and no more than once a year (or after a breach), the information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA, including the Security Overview, its subprocessors' current certifications (Microsoft Azure: ISO 27001, SOC 2; Anthropic: SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001 — as published by them) and answers to a reasonable security questionnaire. If that is not sufficient to meet a legal obligation, the Customer (or an independent auditor bound by confidentiality) may audit Oizn's relevant processing, on 30 days' notice, during business hours, without unreasonably disrupting Oizn's business, and subject to confidentiality. The Customer bears the cost of the audit unless it reveals a material breach of this DPA by Oizn, in which case Oizn bears the reasonable costs.

11. International transfers

11.1 Oizn is established in Canada, which the European Commission has recognized as providing adequate protection for commercial organizations subject to PIPEDA (Decision 2002/2/EC, as confirmed in the Commission's 2024 review). The UK has likewise recognized Canada as adequate.

11.2 Transfers to Subprocessors outside Canada, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland (including Anthropic and Microsoft affiliates in the United States) are made under the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Commission Decision 2021/914, Module 3 processor-to-processor, with the UK International Data Transfer Addendum and Swiss amendments where applicable) that Oizn has in place with those Subprocessors, or under another valid transfer mechanism (such as a Data Privacy Framework certification).

11.3 Where the Customer is in the EEA/UK/Switzerland and the Customer and Oizn agree that the SCCs should apply directly between them, the SCCs are incorporated into this DPA by reference — Module 2 (controller-to-processor) where the Customer is a controller, Module 3 (processor-to-processor) where the Customer is itself a processor — with: Clause 7 (docking) included; Clause 9 Option 2 with the 30-day notice in section 5.2; Clause 11 optional language not included; Clause 13 and Annex I.C: the supervisory authority of the Customer's establishment; Clause 17 Option 1: law of Ireland; Clause 18: courts of Ireland; Annexes I and II: Annexes 1–3 of this DPA. For the UK, the IDTA Addendum (Part 1 Tables completed from this DPA) applies; for Switzerland, references to the GDPR are read as references to the FADP and the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner is the competent authority.

11.4 Processing region controls. The Customer's administrator can set AI processing to US only, in which case Ask Tut requires the provider to confirm US processing and rejects responses that are not. Ask Tut's own records for the hosted edition are stored in Microsoft Azure Canada Central.

12. Liability and precedence

Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations and exclusions in the Agreement, except where Data Protection Laws do not permit that. If this DPA conflicts with the Agreement, this DPA prevails for the processing of Personal Data; if the SCCs apply and conflict with this DPA, the SCCs prevail.

13. Term and changes

This DPA lasts as long as Oizn processes Customer Personal Data. Oizn may update it to reflect changes in Data Protection Laws or in Ask Tut, with 30 days' notice; changes will not reduce the protection afforded to Customer Personal Data.

Annex 3 — Technical and organizational measures

Area Measures
Identity and access Every data call to the Customer's platform is made with the User's own identity (OAuth on-behalf-of / PKCE); the Customer's platform permissions, field security and sharing rules apply. Administrator functions require the platform's administrator role and are enforced server-side. Service identities are least-privilege (e.g., a dedicated "Tut Service" role that can only write usage rows). Oizn staff access to the hosted service is limited to named engineers with multi-factor authentication.
Secrets AI-provider keys and client secrets live in Azure Key Vault and are read by managed identity; never in code, settings pages or logs.
Encryption TLS 1.2+ in transit everywhere. At rest: Azure Storage service encryption (AES-256), Dataverse encryption, Key Vault HSM-backed keys. Refresh tokens stored encrypted.
Change control (data) No write to Customer Data without a User's explicit approval of a signed, single-use approval token (HMAC, 30-minute expiry, re-checked at execution). Administrator rules (tables, fields, status changes, role rules) are enforced by the service, not by the model. Ask Tut has no capability to send email or messages.
Data minimization Only records needed for the question are retrieved and sent to the AI provider; question-text storage can be switched off; conversation state is short-lived; aggregated metrics only for product analytics.
Logging and audit Usage log and change history (before/after) in the Customer-visible Consumption dashboard; Application Insights diagnostics (90 days) without question text; administrator actions are recorded in the platform's own audit log.
Availability and backup Azure zone-redundant storage; Function app on Azure Flex Consumption; the Customer's platform remains the system of record — Ask Tut holds no copy of business data.
Secure development Source in a private GitHub repository with required reviews; dependencies pinned and scanned; automated offline test suites per edition before each release; no secrets in source.
Vulnerability management Azure platform patching; dependency updates monthly or on critical advisories; responsible-disclosure contact support@oizn.com.
Incident response Documented procedure; 48-hour Customer notification; post-incident review.
Personnel Confidentiality obligations; privacy and security training; background checks where lawful for staff with production access.
Subprocessor management Written DPAs; annual review of certifications.
