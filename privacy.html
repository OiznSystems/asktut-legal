Ask Tut — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026 · Version: 1.0

Who we are. Ask Tut is made and operated by Oizn Systems Inc. ("Oizn", "we", "us"), a company incorporated in Ontario, Canada. Registered office: 272 Claridge Dr, Nepean, Ontario K2J 5B9, Canada. Person in charge of the protection of personal information (Privacy Officer): Mo Faheem, privacy@oizn.com · General contact: mo@oizn.com.

This policy explains what personal information Ask Tut handles, why, where it goes and what rights you have. It covers:

  • the Ask Tut products (every edition listed in Schedule A — for Dynamics 365 Customer Service / Customer Engagement, Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations, Dynamics 365 Business Central, SharePoint, HubSpot and Salesforce — and the Ask Tut app for Microsoft Teams), and
  • the asktut.com website.

Plain-English summary first, details after.

1. The short version

  • Ask Tut is an AI assistant that answers questions about your organization's own business data (cases, customers, tickets, orders, documents) as you — it can only see what your sign-in can already see. (Exception: the current HubSpot edition uses the app's installation permissions limited by administrator rules — see Schedule A.)
  • To answer, Ask Tut sends your question and the relevant records to an AI model (Claude, made by Anthropic, or Claude running on Microsoft Foundry if your administrator chooses that). The AI provider does not use your data to train its models.
  • Ask Tut never changes a record without your approval, and it never sends email or messages on your behalf.
  • Ask Tut keeps a usage log (who asked, when, which tools were used, how much it cost) so your organization can control cost and audit changes. Your administrator can turn off storing the text of questions.
  • Depending on the edition and how your organization deployed it, Ask Tut's own data lives inside your organization's own platform (Dataverse, SharePoint) or in Oizn-operated Azure storage in Canada.
  • We don't sell personal information. We don't use it for advertising.

2. Roles: who is responsible for what

Ask Tut is business software bought by an organization (the "Customer") for its staff (the "Users").

  • For the business data Ask Tut reads and writes on the Customer's behalf (the "Customer Data"), the Customer is the controller (under Canadian law, the organization "accountable" for the information) and Oizn is a processor / service provider acting on the Customer's instructions. The Customer's own privacy policy governs that data. If you are a User and have questions about how your employer uses Ask Tut, ask your employer's privacy contact first.
  • For account, billing, support and website information ("Account Data"), Oizn is the controller.

Our processing of Customer Data is governed by our Data Processing Addendum, which forms part of the Terms of Service.

3. What information Ask Tut handles

3.1 Customer Data (your organization's business data)

Category Examples Why
Records Ask Tut reads cases/tickets, accounts, contacts, deals, vendors, items, work orders, knowledge articles, notes, emails and calls linked to a record, SLA timers To answer your question from live data, as you

Ask Tut reads and writes this data through your platform's own APIs, signed in as you (Microsoft Dataverse, Business Central, Finance & Operations, SharePoint/Microsoft Graph, HubSpot, Salesforce). Your platform's permissions, field security and sharing rules apply exactly as they would if you opened the record yourself. Ask Tut does not copy your business data into its own database; it keeps only the log and change-history entries described in 3.3.

3.2 Identity and sign-in data

  • Your name, user principal name / email, user ID, security roles or teams, language and time zone — received from your identity provider (Microsoft Entra ID, HubSpot, Salesforce) when you sign in.
  • Short-lived access tokens issued by your platform so Ask Tut can act as you (on-behalf-of / OAuth). For Salesforce and Teams sign-in, a refresh token is stored encrypted so you don't have to sign in for every question; "sign out" deletes it.

3.3 Ask Tut's own records (usage log, settings, change history)

For every request, Ask Tut records: date/time, user (name and email), channel (side pane, record card, Teams, web), AI model and provider used, where the AI processed it (Global / US only), tokens in/out, tools called, duration, whether an approval was requested / approved / denied, policy refusals, documents produced, errors, and your 👍/👎 feedback with any comment you type.

Question text: by default the text of your question is stored with the log entry so administrators can see what people ask. Your administrator can switch this off ("Privacy → keep questions") — new log entries then contain no question text.

Conversation state: Ask Tut keeps the recent messages of your Teams or web chat so you can ask follow-ups. Conversations are cleared when you say "new chat" and expire automatically (six hours of inactivity in the Teams edition).

Change history: every change you approve is recorded with the request, the "before" values when readable, and the result.

Administrator settings ("policy"): rules, allowed tables and fields, limits, branding, avatar choice. These contain no personal information beyond administrator email addresses used for alerts and per-user cost limits.

3.4 Account Data (Oizn as controller)

Name, work email, company, role, billing details (processed by our payment provider; we never store full card numbers), licence keys, support tickets and the content you include in them, and correspondence with us.

3.5 Website data (asktut.com)

Server logs (IP address, browser, pages visited, timestamps), contact-form submissions, and analytics if enabled (see section 10).

4. Where your data goes (AI processing)

To answer a question, Ask Tut sends an AI provider the following: Ask Tut's instructions, your administrator's rules, your question, the conversation so far, and the records or document excerpts Ask Tut retrieved to answer it. Ask Tut only retrieves what the question needs.

Providers your administrator can choose:

Provider Operated by Where it processes Notes
Claude API (default) Anthropic, PBC (USA) "Global" (Anthropic's regions worldwide) or "US only" (Ask Tut requires the provider to confirm US processing and rejects the answer otherwise) Anthropic's commercial API terms: inputs and outputs are not used to train Anthropic's models; conversation content is not retained by default, except where retention is required by law or content is flagged by Anthropic's automated trust-and-safety systems (then up to two years). Source: Anthropic, "API and data retention" and commercial data retention policy.
Microsoft Foundry (optional) Microsoft, in the Customer's own Azure subscription Global or US Data Zone, as the administrator sets Data is processed under the Customer's own Microsoft agreement; Oizn has no access to it. Anthropic is not involved as a subprocessor.

Canada-only AI processing is not currently offered by any provider for current Claude models; US-only is. We will update this policy if that changes.

Ask Tut does not use your data to train any AI model, and we do not permit our providers to do so.

7. Who we share it with (subprocessors and other recipients)

We share personal information only with:

  • AI provider chosen by the Customer's administrator — Anthropic (Claude API) or Microsoft (Foundry, under the Customer's own agreement).
  • Microsoft Azure (hosting, Key Vault, Application Insights logs, Azure Bot Service for Teams) — Canada Central region for the hosted edition.
  • Your own platform vendor — Microsoft (Dataverse, Dynamics 365, SharePoint, Teams), HubSpot, Salesforce — as the systems Ask Tut acts in on your behalf.
  • Payment and invoicing providers for Account Data.
  • Professional advisers and authorities where the law requires.

The current subprocessor list, with locations and notification of changes, is in the DPA, Annex 2. We never sell personal information and never share it for advertising.

8. International transfers

Oizn is in Canada. The hosted edition runs in Canada Central. AI processing with the Claude API happens in the United States or Anthropic's global regions, as your administrator sets. Where EU/UK personal data is involved, transfers rely on the European Commission's adequacy decision for Canada (for commercial organizations under PIPEDA) and on Standard Contractual Clauses / the UK Addendum with our subprocessors. Details are in the DPA. Where Quebec personal information is involved, the Customer's privacy impact assessment for transfers outside Quebec (Law 25) is supported by the DPA and the Security Overview.

10. Cookies and analytics (asktut.com)

asktut.com uses strictly necessary cookies only, plus — if enabled — privacy-respecting analytics. Where non-essential cookies are used, we ask for your consent first and you can withdraw it at any time. The Ask Tut product itself sets only the session cookies and tokens needed to keep you signed in; it has no advertising or tracking cookies.

11. Security

Ask Tut is designed so it can only do what you can do: every data call is made with your own identity. Other measures include: encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (Azure Storage / Dataverse encryption); secrets in Azure Key Vault; signed one-time approval tokens for changes; administrator-only settings enforced server-side; least-privilege service identities; audit logging of every change; and no outbound email or messaging capability. The Security Overview describes the architecture in detail. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your data we will notify the Customer without undue delay and in any case within the time required by law, and assist with notifications to individuals and regulators.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal information, to data portability, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a privacy regulator (in Canada, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or your provincial regulator, such as Quebec's Commission d'accès à l'information; in the EU/UK, your local supervisory authority; in California, the California Privacy Protection Agency / Attorney General).

  • If the information is Customer Data or Ask Tut's records inside the Customer's environment, contact your organization's administrator — they control it and Oizn will help them respond.
  • For Account Data, email privacy@oizn.com. We respond within 30 days and may ask you to verify your identity.

We do not discriminate against anyone for exercising their rights. We do not "sell" or "share" personal information as those terms are defined in the California Consumer Privacy Act, and we act as a "service provider" for Customer Data.

13. Children

Ask Tut is business software for adults at work. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

14. Automated decisions

Ask Tut produces suggestions and draft content. It does not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you. Every change to a record requires a human's explicit approval.

15. Changes to this policy

We will post changes here with a new effective date and, for material changes, tell Customers at least 30 days before they take effect (by email to the administrator contact or a notice in Tut Admin).
